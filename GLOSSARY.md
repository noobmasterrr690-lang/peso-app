# PESO

Personal Expense & Spending Organizer for students tracking spendable cash, expenses, income, budgets, and savings goals.

## Language

**Balance**:
The user's currently spendable cash and e-wallet money on hand, excluding money set aside inside Savings Goals.
_Avoid_: Wallet, account total, net worth

**Expense**:
A recorded outflow of money from Balance for a purchase or payment on a specific date and Category.
_Avoid_: Transaction, debit, charge

**Income**:
A recorded inflow of money into Balance from an external source (such as allowance, part-time work, scholarship, or gift).
_Avoid_: Deposit, credit, top-up

**Savings Goal**:
A named target amount of money set aside out of Balance for a specific purpose.
_Avoid_: Pot, bucket, fund

**Ledger**:
The single record-keeping authority that records Expenses, Incomes, Savings Goal transfers, and manual Balance corrections while keeping Balance consistent.
_Avoid_: Wallet service, balance manager, transaction service
